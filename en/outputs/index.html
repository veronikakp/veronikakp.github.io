PUBLIC WORK

Selected Outputs

A focused selection of public work connected to Care4Care and my research on working conditions and well-being. This is not intended as a complete academic publication list; the emphasis is on outputs that add useful context to the portfolio.

Peer-reviewed article

Antecedents of Well-Being of Home Care Workers: A Mixed-Methods Systematic Review

The Gerontologist · 2026 · First author · Open access

A theory-informed mixed-methods systematic review integrating evidence from 79 studies on the conditions associated with the well-being of paid home-care workers. The review examines demands and resources across organizational, job and relational contexts and translates the evidence into implications for workforce sustainability and working conditions.

Book chapter

Strengthening Home-Based Care: Practical Recommendations to Support and Value Home Health Aides

CARE4CARE – We Care for Those Who Care, Vol. III · 2026 · First author · Open access

An applied chapter translating evidence on the home-care workforce into practical recommendations aimed at supporting working conditions, recognition and sustainability in home-based care.

Public project reports

Care4Care WP4 — Care Workers’ Well-Being and Rights’ Awareness

Six-country workforce research · Co-author

I co-authored the three public empirical deliverables produced by Care4Care Work Package 4:

D4.2
Quantitative and Qualitative Dataset: Results per Target and Country
D4.3
Country-Based Descriptive Report of Care Workers’ Well-Being
D4.4
Summary of Results with Practical Recommendations

Together, the reports combine quantitative and qualitative evidence from six European countries to describe care workers’ working conditions and well-being and translate the findings into practical recommendations for employers, policymakers and social partners.
