ABOUT

I work at the intersection of organizational psychology, people data and applied research.

My background is in Work & Organizational Psychology. I use quantitative and qualitative evidence to understand working conditions, employee experience and organizational problems, with a particular interest in work that connects analysis with practical HR decisions.

My primary focus is evidence-driven people work across People Analytics, People Insights and Employee Experience, with Organizational Development, HR consulting and competency-based selection as adjacent capabilities.

How I approach people questions

Psychology provides the framework.

It helps define what should be measured, distinguish related constructs and interpret behaviour within the realities of work and organizations.

Data provide evidence.

Depending on the problem, that may mean workforce metrics, surveys, longitudinal data, qualitative material, relational data or multiple sources combined.

Context determines meaning.

A number, correlation, network position or model score is not useful without understanding what it represents—and what it does not.

HR practice is the application.

The purpose is to turn evidence into clearer priorities, better questions and more defensible people decisions.
