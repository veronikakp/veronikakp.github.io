METHODS & CAPABILITIES

Methods & Capabilities

My core approach combines Work & Organizational Psychology with people data, applied research and structured HR methods. The common thread is not a particular tool: it is using the right evidence for the question, interpreting it within its organizational context and being explicit about what the data can and cannot support.

People Analytics & Workforce Insights

I use workforce data to move from descriptive patterns toward evidence that can support HR prioritization. My strongest project evidence in this area includes attrition analysis, employee-experience metrics, dashboarding, statistical testing, exploratory classification and scenario-based estimates of business impact.

I distinguish descriptive concentration from statistical association, and exploratory prediction from models that are strong enough to support individual-level decisions.

HOW I WORK

From evidence to action

Across projects, I use the same basic discipline:

  1. Define the question
  2. Structure the evidence
  3. Analyse the relevant data
  4. Interpret the findings in context
  5. Identify uncertainty and limitations
  6. Translate the evidence into proportionate HR implications or actions

This matters particularly in people decisions, where a technically sophisticated analysis is not useful if the construct is poorly defined, the data do not support the conclusion or the recommended action goes beyond the evidence.
